Format & rules

Your team's format and rules. Both engines follow them for every suite; save your changes, then share them as a rules pack so every suite reads the same.

Team format

The workbook format, coverage, wording and data rules. Built into the generator and the export; Smart also reads this text with every request. Where it disagrees with the Altametrics standard, this wins.
